# Lord Gavelby — Voice approval brief (not a delivered recorded voice)

Desired: mature, wry, cheeky Northern English auctioneer; intelligible British English and consistent pronunciation of guineas, Gavelby, Master, forgery and After Hours.

Nine tutorial chapters and their narration text are supplied in `public/tutorial-chapters.json`. On-screen TV quips remain the approved content from `decks.js`. The optional present-tense voice feature uses a user-device British-English speech engine — it is **not** a fixed voice actor or accent.

For a later production-grade pass, have a narrator audition one chapter, clear rights for commercial game distribution, review a sample, then produce separate per-chapter audio in a consistent recorded voice. Do not automatically enable that voice when the user's Voice control is off.
